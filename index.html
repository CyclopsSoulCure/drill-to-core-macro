Features

Everything the drill-to-core-macro handles for you

Six focused modules that automate the grind while keeping you in control of every toggle.

⛏️

Auto-Drill

Keeps the drill running nonstop, re-engages blocks instantly and never stalls on a missed click, so depth keeps climbing while you are away.

💎

Ore ESP

Highlights valuable ore veins through rock with colour-coded markers and distance labels, so you always know what is worth digging toward.

🌀

Depth Teleport

Jump instantly to saved depths or your deepest checkpoint. Skip the slow descent and resume drilling exactly where the good ore starts.

⚙️

Auto-Upgrade

Spends currency on the best available drill, speed and yield upgrades the moment they become affordable, with a priority order you can tune.

🔁

Fast Reset Loop

Automates the reset cycle — cash out, restart, re-upgrade and dive again — with configurable delays so every run is faster than the last.

🪟

Native Windows App

Runs as a compact Windows 10/11 overlay with hotkeys, per-module toggles and a live status readout. No browser tab, no heavy install.

How it works

From download to deep core in three steps

No complicated setup — the macro is ready to drill within a minute of unzipping.

1

Download & launch

Grab the latest build from gitscore.cc, extract the archive and run the executable. Windows 10 and 11 are supported out of the box — no dependencies to install.

2

Pick your modules

Toggle auto-drill, ore ESP, depth teleport, auto-upgrade and the reset loop from the overlay. Set hotkeys and upgrade priorities the way you like them.
